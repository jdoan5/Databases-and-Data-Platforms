What Tagline is, and the two sources

A small React store, Tagline Supply, pushes GA4's recommended ecommerce events against a written, machine-checked contract. The pipeline unions two GA4 exports, stitches sessions to signed-in people, deduplicates orders, credits each order across the buyer's journey under six models, and checks the tags again on the collected data.

Attribution: who gets the credit under six models

The Spark job credits each order across the buyer's sessions in the 30 days before it, under last click, last non-direct click, first click, linear, time decay (7-day half-life) and position-based (40/20/40). The weights sum to 1 per order and model (the rules, in spark/README.md).
